What we borrow, what we keep

  • RHBig number hero with a live chart, time-range pills, finger-scrub tooltip. Portfolio, each helper, and weekly activity all get it.
  • RHOne action per screen, pinned to the bottom, full width. Second choice is a text link.
  • RHSwipe to confirm for anything that moves money. No accidental taps.
  • RHCustom keypad and quick-pick chips for amounts. Feels like an app, not a form.
  • RHCelebration screens at milestones. Full-bleed colour, short line, confetti.
  • RHRows with sparklines: every list row shows a tiny trend, so you read the page at a glance.
  • HVSerif voice for headlines, numbers and the brand. Robinhood is all-sans; the serif is what keeps us warm and trustworthy.
  • HVPaper, charcoal, sage, clay. Robinhood's neon green and pure black are too loud for mum. We use sage for "up" and clay for "needs you", never red except for danger.
  • SSeals everywhere a name appears. This is ours. Robinhood has nothing like it.
  • SPlain words. "Helpers", "Waiting on you", "sealed". No "pending", no "policy", no "gas".
