Where the mark lives

  • SApp icon. One tile, no wordmark. The dissolving edge is what makes it ours on a shelf of flat squares.
  • SWelcome and loading. Full size on charcoal, stamping in. Tiles drift while something loads.
  • STop bar and sidebar. Small, next to the name, nothing else.
  • SSealed moments. Approval, new helper, first payment. The stamp animation plus a ring.
  • SHelpers. A helper's avatar is the mark in sage. People and places keep the plain charcoal seal: smaller, quieter, so the helper reads as "ours".
  • HVNot everywhere. One mark per screen at most, plus the tiny seals beside names. It should feel rare, like a stamp, not like a logo.
