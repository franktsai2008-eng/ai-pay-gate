Sigil — brand sheet v1

A sigil is a seal: a symbol that proves who stands behind a thing. That is the whole product. Every person, every agent, every transaction carries one. The mark should work as a logo, as a 14px verified badge next to a name, and stamped on a receipt.

Mark concepts

A · Seal lead

Ring and core. The ring is the boundary you set, the core is what's inside it. Reads at any size, already the ◍ from the mockup. Becomes the verified badge unchanged.

A2 · Seal, checked

Same ring with an approval tick. More literal, more "verified". Risk: looks like every other checkmark badge.

B · Two parties

Two rings overlapping, the shared lens filled in. Person and agent, or agent and agent: the sigil lives where they agree. Tells the agent-to-agent story best.

C · Hex seal

Hexagon wax seal with a core. Feels protocol-y and crypto-adjacent. Sharper, less friendly than A.

D · Rune S

A single-stroke S with a point at its heart, like a drawn glyph. Ownable as a monogram, weakest at badge size.

A · on dark, blue core

The core takes the one brand colour. White ring on ink is the app icon.

Recommendation: mark A (Seal) as the primary, B (Two parties) kept as a secondary illustration for the agent-to-agent story on marketing pages. Wordmark in the system sans, semibold, tight tracking, capital S. Blue is spent on exactly one thing, the seal, so when your mom sees blue she knows it means verified.

Voice words: sealed, unsealed, seal it, in your keeping. "Approve" stays "Approve" in the UI; the brand language lives around it, not on the buttons.
