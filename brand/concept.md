# Sigil — brand concept v1

## One line
Sigil is where people and their agents transact as verified peers. Every party carries a seal; nothing moves without one.

## Positioning
- **Category.** Agent-native money. Not a wallet with an AI feature, an economy where humans and agents are both first-class account holders.
- **Promise.** You can see, limit and stop anything your agents do, from your phone, in plain words.
- **Proof.** The seal. One symbol that means "verified, within its terms" for a person, an agent, a business or a transaction.
- **Audience.** Two at once: the average person who wants their AI to handle money without fear, and builders who connect Claude, GPT or Gemini over MCP.

## Name
Sigil: a seal that proves who stands behind a thing. Short, pronounceable, slightly arcane, grounded by a plain UI.

## Mark
**A · Seal.** A ring with a solid core. The ring is the boundary you set, the core is what sits inside it. Same drawing at 120px app icon and 14px verified badge. Blue core on ink for the icon; blue ring and core as the badge. Secondary illustration **B · Two parties**, two rings whose shared lens is filled, for the agent-to-agent story.

## Badge states
- Solid blue ring + core: sealed (verified).
- Dashed grey ring, no core: unsealed. The only time the ring breaks.
- Amber core: sealed but paused.

## Colour
Ink #1C1C1A · Paper #FFFFFF · Tint #F0EFEA · Seal blue #2F6FED (verified only) · Safe #1F8A5B · Needs you #B7791F · Stop #C0392B.

## Type
System sans (SF / Inter), semibold, tight tracking for headings. Tabular figures for money. Mono only for keys and config.

## Visual world
Paper, ink, and one blue light. Soft daylight, matte surfaces, no neon, no 3D coins, no circuit boards. Seals as physical objects: embossed paper, a ring pressed into clay, a single blue dot of light. Calm product photography rather than crypto futurism.

## Voice
Plain, warm, exact. Says what is happening and what you can do. Brand words live around the UI (sealed, in your keeping), never on the buttons ("Approve" stays "Approve").

## Taglines (candidates)
- Money your agents can't misuse.
- Sealed by you.
- Every party verified. Every move in your hand.
