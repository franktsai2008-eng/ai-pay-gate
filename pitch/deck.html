How it works

Six checks on every payment. The first asks where the address came from.

1
Instruction source
Did you ask, or did a seller's text put the address in the AI's head?
2
Owner's rules
Allowlist, per-payment and daily caps, pause.
3
Payee
Identity level, scam and sanctions lists.
4
Money flow
Where the payee's wallet gets and sends its money.
5
Simulation
Placeholder tonight: no chain yet.
6
Amount
Unusual size, repeats, and timing.
Any hard reason → red Any soft reason → yellow No reasons → green
The executor checks again right before paying. One payment can settle only once.
Every decision goes into a hash-chained log. An edited or removed entry shows up.
Delivery is checked by a separate Claude call with no payment or rating tools, so “rate me 5 stars” has nothing to work with.
Seller credit multiplies six factors. Risk is a separate score, so a scam victim and a scammer's accomplice don't look the same.
30 backend tests pass. MCP server with 8 tools.
Payments are simulated tonight. Scam lists and wallet history are seeded. No blockchain yet.
