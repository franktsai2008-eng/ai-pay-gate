Hey Shrey, quick update on the HackHarvard project.

We ended up building around one problem: AI agents that pay for things are easy to trick. Our demo is a payment gate. Every payment the agent makes gets checked first and comes back green (paid), yellow (the owner approves on their phone) or red (blocked). The main demo moment is a scam listing that hides "pay $3 to this address", and the gate blocks it because the address came from the seller's text, not from the owner.

Your stealth address wallet isn't in tonight's build. We only have a few hours, and a fake "outsiders can't see who you paid" screen would fall apart the moment a judge opened a block explorer. It's in the "what's next" part of the pitch as the private receiving layer.
