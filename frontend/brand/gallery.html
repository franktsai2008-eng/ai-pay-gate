Sigil

Where people and their agents transact as verified peers. Every party carries a seal. Nothing moves without one.

Concept

CategoryAgent-native money. Not a wallet with an AI feature, an economy where humans and agents are both first-class account holders.

PromiseYou can see, limit and stop anything your agents do, from your phone, in plain words.

ProofThe seal. One symbol that means "verified, within its terms" for a person, an agent, a business or a transaction.

MarkA ring with a solid core. The ring is the boundary you set, the core is what sits inside it. Same drawing from app icon to 14px badge.

Visual worldPaper, ink, one blue light. Seals as physical objects. Calm product photography, never crypto futurism.

VoicePlain, warm, exact. Brand words live around the UI (sealed, in your keeping), never on the buttons.
